# Manuscript figure package: nested decision trade-off

The five-fold aggregate source is [verified here](NESTED_BAYESIAN_FIVE_FOLD_VERIFIED.md). The figure is generated by [script 91](../scripts/91_plot_nested_decision_tradeoff.py) in [CI](https://github.com/ys30/Ecological-SDM-XGBoost/actions/workflows/nested-decision-figure.yml). It compares pooled outer-fold recall and false-positive rate for three anonymized rare taxa at 10-km nominal buffer and 2% inner negative-class FPR target. A dashed 2% reference line is an **inner selection target**, not a promised outer performance bound.

**Suggested caption:** *Figure X. Rare-taxon detection sensitivity and false-positive burden under nested spatial validation. Points indicate pooled confusion-matrix rates across five held-out spatial folds (not fold-averaged rates), with a 10-km training exclusion and inner-validation negative-class FPR target of 2%. The hierarchical Bayesian and taxon-specific GLM procedures use the same outer/inner spatial partitioning but different probability models. Rare-taxon positives are highly unevenly distributed across folds, so these descriptive rates should not be interpreted as statistically significant model rankings or externally validated deployment performance.*

**Do not claim:** Bayesian consistently reduces FPR, all folds contain positives, 2% outer FPR is guaranteed, nominal km units are formally CRS-validated, or the comparison supports causal attribution. No raw taxon identities should be disclosed.
