# v6.24 buffered C1 protocol

The primary execution in this amendment isolates C1 on the frozen five-fold 4×4 geography with a 10 km outer buffer. The original checkpoint listed two unbuffered outer partitions. Their historical analyses and any subsequent replication are separate from this execution. C2–C4 are separate contrasts and cannot be claimed from C1.

Apply the 10 km great-circle exclusion to outer training plots and compute a 4×4 longitude-by-latitude quartile grid from the retained coordinates. For each occupied cell, validate within that cell and exclude from training every plot within 10 km of any cell validation plot. Both geometries are fixed before class counts. This replaces the v6.22 KMeans inner design. Keep an audit row for every cell. Inner fits require two classes in training. A taxon enters selection when its buffered outer training has at least five positives, two or more inner cells permit fitting, and at least one of those cells contains a validation positive.

Within an eligible taxon and candidate, sum the Bernoulli log losses across prediction rows from all usable inner cells. Divide by the analogous sum for a null using each cell's inner training prevalence, then subtract from one. Retain usable all-negative validation cells. Average skills equally across eligible taxa in that outer fold, then select the maximum and break exact ties by original grid order. This pooled-within-taxon loss ratio replaces the mean of fold-level ratios and avoids giving low-null-loss cells disproportionate influence. Use the 30 deterministic candidates from the v6.22 branch pilot implementation with seed 622. This is a deviation from the prospective PDF’s randomized search over depth, rate, child weight, subsampling and rounds. The pilot grid varies depth, learning rate and rounds while holding the other candidate parameters fixed. Ineligible taxa use the frozen depth-2, rate-0.03, 100-tree XGBoost configuration with minimum child weight 1, subsample 0.8 and column subsample 0.8. All eight taxa remain in matched outer evaluation. Stop if an outer training split is single-class or an outer fold has no eligible selector taxa.

First run an inner-only fold-1 pilot. The dependent full job refits the selected or fallback model and the fixed baseline on identical outer training rows. Preserve keyed OOF probabilities, fold-level diagnostics, candidate scores, software versions, data and fold hashes and training-ID hashes. Probability scores use all paired held-out observations. AUC and AP at taxon-fold level require both observed classes. Pooled cross-taxon rank scores are descriptive.

Report absolute log-loss benefit as comparator minus tuned loss. The v6.24 buffered analysis uses an additional materiality threshold of 0.01 on the absolute log-loss scale. The v6.22 prospective PDF specifies 0.01 AUC, with agreement across two primary designs, for its original primary decision. A single buffered design cannot adjudicate that rule. An interval entirely above 0.01 supports material benefit. An interval entirely inside ±0.01 supports practical equivalence. All other intervals leave materiality unresolved. The tuning seed and refitting seed are varied together for sensitivity at seeds 17, 42, 101, 202, 622 and 2026. Use a paired geographic-block bootstrap with fixed outer-training-independent 4×4 plot-coordinate quartile cells, carrying all taxa and both model predictions for sampled plots. The bootstrap describes test geography conditional on fitted OOF predictions. Report uncertainty across seeds separately.

The prospective PDF also specified training prevalence as the fallback with fewer than five training detections and excluded fallback observations for the primary matched comparison. This amendment keeps fixed-XGBoost fallbacks in the absolute-loss evaluation of the deployed pipeline. The prospective PDF's C2–C4 also included unpenalized GLM, GLMM, and tuning for all three C4 families. Buffered fixed ridge and MaxEnt refits are narrower follow-up contrasts and do not establish completion of those original analyses.
